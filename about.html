Who we are

Ogle and Hassan Associates

Ogle and Hassan Associates is a full-service Kenyan certified public accounting firm providing innovative, cut-through audit and consultancy services in accountancy, taxation, public finance advisory, and business advisory.

The firm is registered as an audit firm with the Institute of Certified Public Accountants of Kenya (ICPAK). That registration reflects our commitment to professional standards and trusted practice.
