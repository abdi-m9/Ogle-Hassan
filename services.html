Our services

Expert guidance for your financial and compliance needs

We offer a range of services delivered by experienced professionals who help clients find practical solutions for their businesses.

01

Accountancy, bookkeeping & payroll

Ogle & Hassan Associates, CPA (K) provides accounting, payroll management, and bookkeeping services to clients in Kenya and the Horn of Africa. We enable you to focus more on your core activities while we keep your records accurate and up to date.

  • Day-to-day bookkeeping and account maintenance
  • Payroll management and related reporting
  • Flexible support for growing teams and operations
02

Auditing & assurance

At Ogle & Hassan Associates, you will always be allocated a manager within the firm to manage your requirements. The relationship manager is responsible for educating staff on your audit about the unique characteristics of your business.

  • Dedicated relationship manager for your engagement
  • Audit approach tailored to how your business works
  • Assurance that supports stakeholder confidence
03

Taxation

When it comes to tax planning and strategy, every case is unique. Our approach remains versatile, with an awareness that individual issues must be dealt with on their own merits - but with overall, long-term considerations always in mind.

  • Tax planning and strategy suited to your situation
  • Support with compliance and filings
  • Advice that balances immediate needs with long-term position
04

Business advisory

Our talented professionals provide cost-effective, value-added solutions to improve the financial position and operations of your business. Our advisory team provides solutions based on long-term industry trends and your organisation's interests.

  • Operational and financial improvement recommendations
  • Advisory aligned with industry trends
  • Support for growth while managing risk and compliance
05

Financial consulting

Your accounting requirements are handled professionally and stay up to standard. We are flexible in our solutions and can be contracted on a project basis to clean up your accounts, on a short-term arrangement, or on fixed monthly billing.

  • Account clean-up projects
  • Short-term financial support
  • Ongoing monthly arrangements
06

Public finance advisory

As part of our full consultancy offering, we provide public finance advisory alongside accountancy, taxation, and business advisory - helping clients navigate complex financial and regulatory environments with clarity.

  • Public finance advisory support
  • Guidance aligned with professional standards
  • Clear recommendations for decision-makers
